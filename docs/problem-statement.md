## Background

Electricity grids depend on important equipment such as **transformers and substations**. If one of these machines develops a problem, it can lead to equipment damage or even a power outage.

These machines already produce useful information such as **temperature, vibration, load, oil condition, and other sensor readings**. Weather conditions and past failures can also increase the chance of a problem.

The challenge is that this information is often available in different places and is not always used together early enough to prevent a failure.

## The Problem

A transformer may look normal today but show signs that it could fail in the future.

Traditional maintenance often works on a **fixed schedule**. This means a machine may be inspected because its maintenance date has arrived, even when another machine is actually at higher risk.

The main problem is:

```text
Machine Data
     +
Weather Data
     +
Past Failure Data
     ↓
Not Combined Quickly
     ↓
Risk May Be Missed
     ↓
Equipment Failure
     ↓
Power Outage
```

PRAVAHA solves this by combining these different types of information and checking the condition of **each machine separately**.

## Who is Affected

### Primary Users

**Power-grid operators and maintenance teams**

They need to know which transformer or substation is becoming risky so they can act before it fails.

### Other Affected Users

**Industrial companies and factories** can face production delays when electricity is interrupted.

**Electricity consumers** can also be affected when equipment failures cause outages.

## Why It Matters

A single failure in important grid equipment can cause:

```text
Equipment Damage
        ↓
Emergency Repair
        ↓
Downtime
        ↓
Financial Loss
        ↓
Power Disruption
```

The challenge gives a figure of **$1M+ per hour** for the cost of some power-transformer and substation failures, with the potential to affect millions of people.

PRAVAHA aims to help operators act earlier by answering three simple questions:

```text
Which machine is at risk?
          ↓
How serious is the risk?
          ↓
What should we do now?
```

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Many existing maintenance processes rely mainly on **scheduled or calendar-based maintenance**.

At the same time, useful information may be separated across:

```text
Sensor Data       Weather Data       Failure Records
     ↓                 ↓                    ↓
     └─────────────────┼────────────────────┘
                       ↓
              Difficult to combine
                       ↓
                Delayed decisions
```

This makes it harder to identify the **right machine at the right time**.

PRAVAHA brings these inputs together, calculates the risk for each asset, and turns the result into:

```text
Risk Score
    ↓
Alert
    ↓
Maintenance Recommendation
    ↓
Crew Pre-positioning
    ↓
Preventive Action
```
